Designing a football player guessing round

Football data looks simple until a guessing game has to accept real fan answers. Club names change, players move during a season, and the same person may be known by a full name, surname, nickname, or transliteration. I keep a canonical player record and attach accepted answer aliases to it instead of comparing directly with the display name.

Use dated clues

A clue such as current club needs a timestamp. Career clubs and national team are more stable, but they still need a clear definition. I prefer a clue ladder that moves from nationality and role to league history, club history, and then a recognisable achievement.

Make wrong guesses informative

A useful wrong guess should narrow the next attempt. Position, nationality region, career era, and competition history can be compared without exposing the answer. The result should be readable at a glance and should not require fans to decode an unexplained colour system.

Small technical choices

Normalize whitespace and punctuation, but do not strip meaningful name characters. Cache the daily record, keep the answer on the server or in a signed payload when practical, and make the share text spoiler-free. Those details have more effect on trust than an elaborate animation.
